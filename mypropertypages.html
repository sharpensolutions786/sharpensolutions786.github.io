A web page for every property, and every lead to your phone

My Property Pages gives an estate agent a public profile page and a separate shareable page for every property, each with a photo gallery, the details, a downloadable PDF detail sheet and an enquiry form. An enquiry arrives as a push notification, gets an instant thank-you reply, and lands in a small CRM with stages, notes and follow-up dates. It runs on the web and on Android, and the free plan includes two property pages with no card.

Why it is priced per account

Every other single-property website builder bills per listing, so ten properties can cost between $100 and $290 a month. The pricing page shows the sourced comparison; this one is priced per account with a generous property cap instead.
