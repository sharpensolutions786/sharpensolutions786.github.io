Crypto price alerts without handing over account access

A price alert should watch the market and reach you — it should not need your exchange API key or a wallet connection. If an alert app asks for those, it can reach your positions; most people never need to grant that.

The app we use

Coin Price Alert asks for neither — no exchange key, no wallet, no seed phrase — so it cannot move your funds. It covers price levels, percentage moves, trailing stops, RSI, volume spikes and MACD/EMA crossovers, delivered by push, Telegram or email. Ten active alerts are free.

No install needed

There is a browser version if you would rather set alerts from a desktop and skip the app store.
