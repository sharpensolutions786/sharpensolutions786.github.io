Free property paperwork, and a web page for every listing

My Property Pages gives an estate agent — or a private seller — a shareable web page for every property, with a photo gallery, the details, a downloadable PDF sheet and an enquiry form that sends the lead straight to the owner's phone. It also keeps a free library of property document checklists and templates that anyone can use.

The free document library

The free property document checklists cover the paperwork people forget at the worst moment — what to collect, in what order, and why each item matters.

The ones people look for most

  • Rent receipt format — the fields a receipt needs to be accepted for an HRA claim, with a ready template.
  • Home loan document checklist — everything a lender asks for, grouped so nothing is missed.
  • Documents needed to sell a property — the title, tax and society papers a buyer's lawyer will want.

The product itself is free to start — two property pages on the free plan, and it is priced per account rather than per listing, which is unusual for this kind of tool.
