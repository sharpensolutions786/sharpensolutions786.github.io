An invoice app that works offline, with no account

Most invoice apps want an account before you can type anything, and many stop working when the signal drops. EstimateBill is built the other way round: every invoice, estimate and client lives in a database on the phone itself, so there is no signup, no server holding your customer list, and no internet needed to make or export a document.

It sends branded PDFs by WhatsApp, email or print, marks them paid in one tap, keeps a per-client ledger, and sets the right tax name and rate for your country (GST, VAT, SST and sales tax across 58 countries). Version 1.0 is free with every feature unlocked, no ads and no in-app purchases.

No install needed

There is also a free invoice generator in the browser if you just need one document.
